DISCOVER EXCLUSIVITY

An exceptionally beautiful resort in the picturesque Indian town of Mala, Giby Farm is the ideal private retreat for those seeking to get away from hectic schedules.

Our two private cottages come with spectacular water views on ten acres that can be all yours if you book both cottages together. To add to the flavour, we offer customised and sumptuous culinary experiences that feature fresh local produce and authentic Kerala cuisines. The expertly landscaped gardens are ideal for MICE, with stylish indoor and outdoor spaces for corporate or personal events.

The Giby Farm promise is the ultimate luxury of privacy: there is nothing between you and the Mala River that borders three sides of the property and keeps it away from the public eye. There’s only water, skies and emerald greenery as far as the eye can see. It’s a perfect balance of privacy, comfort and refined opulence in truly verdant splendour.
